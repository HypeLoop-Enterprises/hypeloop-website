ABOUT US

Who we are

HypeLoop is an independent game studio on Roblox, founded in 2026 by two game makers who grew up playing the games we now build.

We are small on purpose. Every game has one owner who designs it, builds it and stays with it after launch. That keeps decisions fast and quality high, and it means the people reading your feedback are the same people shipping the next update.

We build in public with our community. Group members test new features early, vote on ideas and see their suggestions show up in the games.

OUR VISION

Build the Roblox games people come back to every day, not because they have to, but because they want to.

HOW WE BUILD

Our promises to players

These rules apply to every HypeLoop game. If we ever break one, call us out.

Fair to play

Paid items never decide who wins. Anything random you can buy shows its odds up front.

No pressure

No fake countdowns, no tricks to make you spend, no rewards for likes or favorites.

Regular updates

New content, events and fixes on a steady schedule. A game is never finished at launch.

We listen

Feedback from the community shapes what we fix and build next. Group members get a vote.

FAQ

Questions

Are HypeLoop games free to play?

Yes. Every game is free on Roblox. Optional purchases exist, but you can play, progress and win without them.

How do I join the HypeLoop group?

Open our community page on Roblox and press Join. It is free and instant. Members get in-game perks and early access to new games.

What do group members get?

Group-only rewards inside our games (for example extra income and free daily items in Catch & Steal), early access to new games and a vote on what we build next.

When does your next game come out?

When it is fun enough to be worth your time. We do not announce dates we cannot keep. Group members will hear first and get to play first.

Where can I follow updates?

Join the Roblox group and follow us on TikTok at @hypeloopgames for clips, sneak peeks and update news.

Is HypeLoop part of Roblox?

No. We are an independent studio that makes games on the Roblox platform. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
